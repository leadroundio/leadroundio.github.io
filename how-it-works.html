faq

Common questions

What is Leadround?

A peer-to-peer lead marketplace for service professionals who work with software products: agencies, consultants and freelancers. Members refer the leads they cannot take to fellow experts who can, and earn a referral fee.

Who can join?

Any agency, consultant or freelancer who works with clients on software products such as Zapier, n8n, Airtable, Klaviyo, Pipedrive, HubSpot or Shopify.

Which leads can I refer?

Leads that booked a call with you and did not work out: no capacity for new work, a tool or skill you do not specialise in, a location you do not serve, no availability for the project duration, a budget mismatch, or simply not a fit.

How much do I earn for a lead?

You choose one of two options when you post the lead. A fixed referral fee, typically $50 to $500, for each introduction, with up to three introductions per lead. Or a 10% commission on the final project value, earned when the lead converts and the payment is collected from the client.

Does Leadround check the leads?

Yes. Each lead submission is verified: our staff contacts the client to make sure the request is real, accurate and current.

Who takes the leads I refer?

Other Leadround members who specialise in the tool the client needs. They ask to be introduced and Leadround makes the introduction, to no more than three experts per lead.

What does a lead look like?

One client, one request, your notes from the conversation: what they need, which tools, roughly when and for what budget. The client's contact details are shared only with the experts who are introduced.

Does Leadround sell lead lists?

No. Every lead is a single real client request from a service professional who spoke with the client. No lists, no scraped contacts, no cold data.
