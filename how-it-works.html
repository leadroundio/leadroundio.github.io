faq

Common questions

What is Leadround?

Leadround is a peer-to-peer lead marketplace for service professionals who work with software products: agencies, consultants and freelancers. Members sell the client requests they cannot take and buy the ones that fit them.

Who can join?

Any agency, consultant or freelancer who works with clients on software products such as Zapier, n8n, Airtable, Klaviyo, Pipedrive, HubSpot or Shopify. One account covers both buying and selling.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Joining and browsing leads are free. Fees apply only when a lead changes hands, and the current terms are always shown inside the app before you act.

How do I sell a lead?

Post the client request you cannot take. Leadround checks it, other experts ask to be introduced, and you earn a referral fee when the introduction is made.

How do I buy a lead?

Browse requests by tool and specialty, request an introduction to the ones that fit you, and get connected to the client.

Does Leadround sell lead lists?

No. Every lead is a single real client request from a service professional who spoke with the client. There are no lists, no scraped contacts and no cold data.
