why here

Why freelancers prefer referred leads

On freelance marketplaces the client posts a form and fifty people reply. The work goes to whoever is cheapest or fastest. A referred lead is different: someone the client trusts already explained the project, confirmed the budget and said your kind of specialist is the right fit. The conversation starts with trust instead of a price war.

faq

Common questions

Do I need an agency to join?

No. Independent consultants and freelancers are the core of Leadround. You need a real specialty in a software product and clients you can serve.

What kind of projects come through?

Implementation, integration, automation, migration, setup and ongoing management work on business software: CRMs, email platforms, ecommerce stacks, automation tools and no-code builders.

Do I have to pay to join?

No. Joining and browsing are free. Fees apply when a lead changes hands, and the current terms are shown inside the app.

What if I do not have leads to sell?

Start as a buyer. The leads you cannot take will come, and when they do you will have a place to send them.
