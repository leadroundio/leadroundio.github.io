Privacy Policy

last updated Nov 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Leadround, Inc. ("Leadround", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares personal information when you visit leadround.io or use the Leadround platform at app.leadround.io (together, the "Platform"), and the choices you have about that information.

Leadround is a marketplace where service professionals ("members") post client requests ("leads") they cannot take and are introduced to other members who can. This Privacy Policy covers personal information about members and about the clients described in leads.

By using the Platform you agree to this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy is part of our Terms of Service.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you create an account, complete your profile, post a lead, request an introduction, make or receive a payment, or contact us, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, business details, payment and payout details, and the content of your messages.

Information in leads. A lead may contain personal information about a client: contact details and a description of the project.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Platform we and our service providers may collect information about your device, browser and how you use the Platform through cookies and similar technologies. See "Cookies" below.

Information from other sources. We may receive information about you from other members, for example when a member posts a lead or requests an introduction, and from publicly available sources such as your business website.

How we use information

We use personal information to:
• provide, operate and maintain the Platform;
• create and manage accounts and verify members;
• publish leads, process introduction requests and make introductions;
• process payments and payouts and keep the records the law requires;
• send service messages about your account, leads, introductions and payments;
• send marketing communications, which you can opt out of at any time;
• respond to your questions and provide support;
• understand how the Platform is used and improve it;
• protect the Platform, our members and others from fraud, abuse and violations of our Terms of Service;
• comply with legal obligations.

Where required by law, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases: performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests, your consent, and compliance with legal obligations.

How we share information

With other members. Information in a lead is shown to members so they can decide whether to request an introduction. A client's contact details are shared only with the member who is introduced to that client. Members who take part in an introduction see each other's name and business details.

With service providers. We share information with third-party companies that perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, payment processing, email delivery, customer support and analytics. These companies may use the information only to provide those services to us.

For legal reasons. We may disclose information if we believe it is necessary to comply with the law or a legal process, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Leadround, our members or others.

Business transfers. If Leadround is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of all or part of its business, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

With your consent. We may share information for other purposes with your consent.

We do not sell personal information.

Cookies

We and our service providers use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, understand how the Platform is used and measure the effectiveness of our communications. Some of these technologies are provided by third-party analytics services.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. If you block cookies that are needed to sign in, parts of the Platform will not work.

Your choices

Account information. You can review and update your profile information in your account settings or by contacting us.

Marketing communications. You can opt out of marketing emails by using the unsubscribe link in any such email or by contacting us. We will still send you service messages about your account.

Leads about you. If you are a client whose information was posted on Leadround by a member, you can ask us to review, correct or delete that information by contacting us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@leadround.io. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

California residents. California law gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to request its deletion or correction, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide the Platform, to comply with our legal obligations, to resolve disputes and to enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed for these purposes we delete it or make it anonymous.

Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Leadround is based in the United States and personal information is processed there. If you use the Platform from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those of your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Third-party websites

The Platform may contain links to websites and services that we do not operate, including the websites of members and clients. This Privacy Policy does not apply to them. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any website or service you visit.

Children

The Platform is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of this page. If we make material changes we will notify you through the Platform or by email before they take effect. Your continued use of the Platform after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at support@leadround.io.
