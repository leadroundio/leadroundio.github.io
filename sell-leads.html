where to sell

Where to sell leads, and where not to

Lead brokers buy lists and resell the same contact to as many buyers as will pay, which is bad for the client and bad for your name. Leadround is built for the opposite case: one real client you spoke with, verified by our staff and introduced to no more than three specialists who asked for the introduction. Whether you sell web design leads, SEO leads, automation leads or any other service request, the right place for it is a fellow professional, not a list vendor.

faq

Common questions

What counts as a lead I can sell?

A real client who asked you for help with a project and whom you cannot or do not want to take. One request, one client, your own notes. Lists, scraped contacts and cold prospects are not leads on Leadround.

Which leads sell best?

Clear requests for work on a software product: implementation, automation, integration, migration or ongoing management, with a named tool, a timeline and a realistic budget. The more specific your notes, the faster a specialist asks for the lead.

How much can I earn per lead?

You choose one of two options when you post the lead. A fixed referral fee, typically $50 to $500, for each introduction, with up to three introductions per lead. Or a 10% commission on the final project value. Use the calculator above for a rough estimate.

How do I get paid?

You connect a payout account in the app. A fixed fee is earned for each introduction to your lead. A commission is earned when your referral converts into a project and the payment is collected from the client.

What does it cost to sell a lead?

Posting a lead costs nothing. The referral fee is paid by the expert who is introduced to your client.

Does Leadround check the leads?

Yes. Each lead submission is verified: our staff contacts the client to make sure the request is real, accurate and current.

Who takes the leads I post?

Fellow Leadround members who specialise in the tool the client needs: agencies, consultants and freelancers. Your lead is shown to them, not broadcast to the internet.

Is my client's contact information safe?

Yes. Members see the project, the tools, the timing and the budget range, not who the client is. Contact details are shared only with the experts who are introduced.

What happens to the client?

Leadround introduces the client to the experts who asked for the introduction, and a project manager stays with the client to help the project get delivered.

What if nobody takes my lead?

You can update the lead or withdraw it at any time from your account.

Do I have to buy leads too?

No. You can join Leadround only to sell the leads you cannot take.
