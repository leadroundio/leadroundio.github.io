why sell

A declined lead is still worth something

It takes real work to get a client on a call. When the project is not for you, the usual options are to say no, or to pass the client to a friend for free and hope the favour comes back. Leadround turns that handover into a transaction with a known price, a verified counterpart and a payment that actually arrives.

Selling a lead also keeps you in the client's good books. They asked you for help and you found them the right person. The world runs on referrals, and this one has your name on it.

faq

Common questions

What counts as a lead I can sell?

A real client who asked you for help with a project and whom you cannot or do not want to take. One request, one client, your own notes. Lists, scraped contacts and cold prospects are not leads on Leadround.

How much do I earn?

You set the terms when you post a lead and the current fee options are shown in the app. Payment is made when the introduction happens.

Do I have to be a buyer too?

No. Many members start by selling. Most end up doing both, because the colleague who bought your Klaviyo lead is the one who will send you a Zapier project next month.

What happens to the client?

Leadround introduces the client to the expert who requested the introduction and stays in the loop to make sure the handover goes well.
