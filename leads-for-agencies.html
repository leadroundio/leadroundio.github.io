How agencies use Leadround

The sales team posts the requests that did not pass qualification: too small, wrong stack, wrong country, no capacity this quarter. The delivery leads watch for requests in their stack and request introductions to the ones that fit. Both sides run on one agency account.

The result is a lead pipeline that does not depend on ads or cold outreach, built from the calls agencies like yours are already having.

faq

Common questions

Is Leadround a lead generation agency?

No. Leadround is a marketplace. The leads are posted by other service professionals, not generated by ads or scraped from the web. Fees apply when a lead changes hands; the current terms are shown in the app.

Can several people from my agency use it?

Yes. Sales can post leads your agency declines while delivery teams buy the ones that fit. Ask support about setting this up for your team.

Can we sell leads without buying?

Yes. Many agencies join only to monetise declined leads and start buying later.

How is this different from Upwork or Bark?

Nobody bids. Each lead is one real client request from a fellow professional, checked by Leadround and introduced to a small number of experts, not auctioned to the crowd.
