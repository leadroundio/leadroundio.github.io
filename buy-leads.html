why here

Not a job board, not a lead list

On a job board you bid against dozens of providers for a client who posted a form. From a lead vendor you buy a contact that was sold to several others at the same time. On Leadround a lead is one client who asked a fellow expert for help, was turned away for a good reason, and is now waiting for the right specialist.

Every lead is checked by Leadround before it goes live, and the number of experts introduced to one client stays small. You are not one of fifty replies.

Common questions

Where do the leads come from?

From Leadround members: agencies, consultants and freelancers who spoke with a client and could not take the project. Each lead is one real request with the seller's notes attached.

How much does a lead cost?

Fees apply when a lead changes hands, and the current terms are always shown inside the app before you act.

How many experts get the same lead?

A small number. Leads are never broadcast to everyone.

Can I get notified about new leads in my specialty?

Yes. Choose the tools you work with and Leadround will tell you when a matching request appears.
